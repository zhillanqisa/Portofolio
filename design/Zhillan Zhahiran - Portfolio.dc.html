Studio Proses Perencanaan, Kecamatan Kroya, Kabupaten Cilacap

Group D3
Map coordinator · supervisor Dr.-Ing. Prihadi Nugroho, S.T., M.T., M.P.P.

A planning study read at three nested scales: the whole sub-district, the urban area, and a 72-hectare site across Desa Kedawung and Desa Bajing. The work profiles demography, economy, physical conditions and infrastructure, then traces each scale to its own issue tree: Kroya Station underused as a strategic transport node, fragmented trade and mobility in the urban core, and a site whose circulation and visual identity remain unresolved. All maps in the folders above were produced for this studio.

Powering a Resilient Future: Sayung Village, Demak

13–24 July 2026
Summer Workshop, Faculty of Engineering UNDIP × University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa · Group 2

A bowl-shaped coastal basin sitting below river level, where industrial groundwater extraction drives subsidence and tidal water mixes with sewage and household waste. Group 2 mapped land change from 2017 to 2025, built a problem tree and SWOT, and set out a roadmap around solar-powered drainage pumps: raised floors and rain catchment in the short term, replacing groundwater extraction with purified surface water and community-based ecosystem restoration in the long term.
